User manual

A simple guide to preparing data, importing it, and reading your dashboard.

Your first dashboard, step by step

Get started: prepare a file, upload it, and explore what your numbers mean.

STEP 01

Prepare your data

In Excel or Google Sheets, name each column, for example Month, Sales, Orders. Put one record in each row.

STEP 02

Save it as a CSV

Download your file as CSV (.csv). Enter numbers plainly, like 1200; leave out currency signs and thousands separators.

STEP 03

Upload your file

Choose Upload a CSV, or drag and drop the file onto this page. Your data preview appears below.

STEP 04

Explore your numbers

Review totals and averages above. Choose a measure from the dropdown, then switch between line and bar charts. Select Show all rows to see your full dataset.

Remember: Last 3/6 periods shows the last rows, so sort your CSV from oldest to newest first. Data stays in this tab for now; refreshing restores the sample data. Do not upload sensitive data.

Sign in before importing to save each CSV in your account. Without an account, imported data stays only in this browser tab.
